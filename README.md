# Privacy Policy for ZeroPath - EMI Tracker

**Last Updated**: January 25, 2026

## Introduction

ZeroPath ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our EMI Tracker mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide
- **Loan Information**: Loan amounts, interest rates, tenure, EMI amounts, and payment dates
- **Payment Records**: History of EMI payments you mark as paid
- **User Preferences**: App settings, theme preferences, currency selection
- **Optional Data**: User name (if provided), profile information

### Automatically Collected Data
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, app version
- **Usage Data**: Features used, screen views, interaction patterns (anonymized)
- **Notification Data**: Device tokens for local notification scheduling

### Data We DO NOT Collect
- ❌ We do NOT access your bank accounts
- ❌ We do NOT collect your actual payment transactions
- ❌ We do NOT share your data with third parties
- ❌ We do NOT sell your information

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
1. **Loan Tracking**: Store and manage your EMI and loan data locally on your device
2. **Reminders**: Schedule local notifications for upcoming EMI payments
3. **Analytics**: Track app performance and improve user experience (anonymized)
4. **Backup**: Enable you to backup your data to Google Drive (optional)

### Legal Basis
We process your data based on:
- Your consent when you use the app
- Legitimate interest in providing and improving our services
- Contractual necessity to deliver app features

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All loan and payment data is stored **locally on your device** using SQLite database
- Data is encrypted at rest using Android/iOS security features
- App lock (PIN/Biometric) adds additional security layer

### Cloud Backup (Optional)
- If enabled, encrypted backups are stored in **your personal Google Drive**
- We do NOT have access to your Google Drive
- You can delete backups anytime from your Google Drive

### Security Measures
- ✅ Data encryption at rest
- ✅ Secure storage for sensitive data (PIN, biometric data)
- ✅ No data transmission to external servers (except optional Google Drive backup)
- ✅ Regular security updates

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT Share Your Data
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information
- We do not share data with advertisers
- We do not disclose data to third parties

### Exceptions (Legal Requirements)
We may disclose information if required by:
- Law enforcement or legal process
- Protection of our legal rights
- Prevention of fraud or security threats

## Your Rights

### Data Control
You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all your stored data within the app
- **Export**: Export your data as PDF or CSV
- **Delete**: Delete individual loans or all data (Nuclear Wipe)
- **Backup**: Create encrypted backups to Google Drive
- **Restore**: Restore data from previous backups

### Data Portability
- Export all data in standard formats (JSON, CSV, PDF)
- Transfer data to other apps or services

### Data Deletion
To delete all your data:
1. Go to Settings → Nuclear Wipe
2. Confirm deletion
3. All local data will be permanently deleted
4. Manually delete Google Drive backups if applicable

## Children's Privacy

ZeroPath is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child has provided us data, please contact us.

## Third-Party Services

### Services We Use
1. **Google Drive API** (Optional - for cloud backup only)
   - Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
   - Only if you enable cloud backup
   - We do not access your Drive data

2. **Firebase Analytics** (Anonymized usage data)
   - Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
   - Used to improve app experience
   - Can be opted out in Settings

3. **Firebase Crashlytics** (Crash reporting)
   - Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
   - Helps us fix bugs and crashes
   - No personal data collected

### No Advertising
- We do NOT use advertising SDKs
- We do NOT show ads (subscription-based model)

## Data Retention

- **Active Use**: Data retained as long as you use the app
- **App Uninstall**: All local data deleted when you uninstall
- **Backups**: Google Drive backups retained until you manually delete them
- **Analytics**: Anonymized usage data retained for 24 months

## International Data Transfers

- All data stored locally on your device (in your country)
- Optional Google Drive backups stored in Google's data centers (your region)
- No cross-border data transfers by us

Your continued use after changes indicates acceptance.
